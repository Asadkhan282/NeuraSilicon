# NeuraSilicon

**Engineering intelligence into silicon.**

NeuraSilicon is an open engineering initiative focused on the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, GPU Architecture, VLSI, digital hardware, semiconductor systems, and high-performance computing**.

## Mission

Build, study, and share practical hardware-software systems that push efficient AI computation from algorithms toward silicon.

## Core Focus Areas

- **AI & LLM Systems** — efficient inference, quantization, model acceleration, and deployment.
- **GPU Architecture** — kernels, memory systems, parallel compute, and performance optimization.
- **VLSI & Digital Design** — RTL, FPGA/ASIC design, verification, arithmetic units, and accelerator blocks.
- **Semiconductor Engineering** — silicon architectures, device-aware design, reliability, and emerging compute.
- **Hardware–Software Co-Design** — connecting models, compilers, runtimes, and hardware for end-to-end efficiency.

## Current Direction

NeuraSilicon is being developed as a public technical portfolio and research platform for:

1. GPU and AI workload optimization
2. Low-precision arithmetic and efficient compute
3. RTL/VLSI accelerator design
4. Semiconductor and silicon architecture research
5. Open engineering experiments and reproducible benchmarks

See the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md) for planned development.
